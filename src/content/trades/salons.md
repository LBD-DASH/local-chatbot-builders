## What the chatbot does for a salon website

The chatbot is built around the information you give us. We write the answers with you, you approve them, and the bot only uses approved answers. Anything it cannot answer is passed to you.

## How it works

1. You send us the details of your business: services, hours, location, policies.
2. We draft the FAQ answers and the enquiry questions, and you approve them.
3. We set up the chatbot and give you the snippet to add to your website.
4. Enquiries reach you in the channel we agree on.
